The energy

What Sagittarius season actually means

JUPITER
The bigger view, the bigger vessel
  • Sagittarius season runs November 22 to December 21, ending at the winter solstice.
  • It's ruled by Jupiter: abundance, expansion, the bigger view, opportunity. The energy of vision, faith, teaching, freedom, truth-telling and taking a bigger swing.
  • In Kabbalistic astrology this is Kislev, the month of miracles. Its work is correcting doubt: not by pretending you're certain, but by taking a grounded action that expresses trust while the outcome is still unseen.
  • Kislev is connected to the Concealed Light (Or HaGanuz): the idea that support, answers and possibility can already be there before you can see them.
  • The teaching isn't about manifesting anything. It's about acting from faith while you do the work, and expanding your capacity to receive.

From depth and release to vision and direction
  • Audit what's no longer true, then name the bigger mission. Where are we going? What is the vision?
  • Launch, pitch, sell, create, be seen. It doesn't have to be perfect.
Visibility
Where am I hiding my message or minimizing my authority?

Stop waiting to feel ready. Say who you help and what you believe in your bio, your about page and your next post.

Content
What do I actually believe about my work?

Make one bold piece of thought-leadership content that states your philosophy, not just a tip.

Offers
What transformation am I under-naming?

Nobody cares about the product. They care about the transformation. Lead with the result and the identity shift, then the deliverables.

Sales + invitations
What would I do if I trusted the right people are already looking for this?

Reopen one stalled conversation, pitch one collaborator, invite someone to a call, make one clear offer. No forcing.

Pricing + receiving
Where are doubt, over-giving or fear of being "too much" keeping me from receiving?

Raise one price, stop one freebie that should be paid, or say yes to the help that's being offered.

Capacity
Do I have room to hold more?

Expansion needs space: in your calendar, your emotions, your energy and your operations. Clear one thing before you add one.

Systems, after Nov 25
What structure would make this expansion sustainable?

Turn the vision into a calendar, an SOP, an onboarding flow, an offer or a launch plan. Make it solid, make it real.

Four questions before you say yes
  • As a projector, it's about recognition and invitation, and understanding that not all invitations are aligned invitations.
  • How do I feel about it? How excited am I about it?
  • Run any opportunity, client or offer through four questions: Do I have the desire? The capacity? Is the timing right? Does it fit my mission?
  • Stop trying to force yourself into things that seem like they should be aligned, but really aren't.
  • Then decide the way your design decides. Your authority is on your expansion card.
